# Custom shaders - reference map

Reference for the project's hand-written shaders. This is a **how-it-works map**, not a progress
log - history lives in `CHANGELOG.md`, and per-shader design intent lives in the banner comment at
the top of each `.shader`. Keep this current when a shader's role, dependencies, or hard limits
change; don't duplicate the code.

All shaders target **URP 17 / Unity 6 (6000.x)**, Forward. ASCII-English only (project rule).

The staged realism roadmap for the experimental water is in
`REALISTIC_WATER_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`.

## Water shaders

There are **two** water shaders and they are independent - editing one never touches the other.

### `Market/World/StylizedWater` - the shipped cartoon water

- **File:** `Assets/_Project/Art/Materials/Water/MarketWater.shader`
- **Material:** `M_Ocean.mat` (also references `WaterFlow.png` as `_FlowMap` and a caustic map).
- **Look:** bright, readable cartoon sea that must read as water from *any* angle - depth-graded
  color, animated wave normals, crisp toon sun glint, sky fresnel, alpha blend, shoreline foam.
- **Transparent** (`Blend SrcAlpha OneMinusSrcAlpha`, `ZWrite Off`).
- Deliberately does **not** sample main-light shadow attenuation - doing so produced a
  camera-following dark circle at the shadow cascade boundary (see `CHANGELOG` "Fixed"). Depth
  sampling stays on only for shoreline foam.
- **Where it still runs:** `Map.unity` only. `Island.unity` no longer uses it - its water was
  swapped to the imported Bitgem stylized water (`Art/Materials/Water/StylizedWater_01..03.mat`,
  project copies of the package materials, bound by `IslandSceneBuilder`). Which water ships on the
  Island - this shader, Bitgem, or the realistic candidate - is still an open call.

### `Market/World/RealisticWater` - experimental PBR-ish water

- **File:** `Assets/_Project/Art/Materials/Water/RealisticWater.shader`
- **Material:** `M_RealisticWaterLab.mat`. **Test bed:** `WaterShaderLab.unity` only.
- **Runs on a dense grid mesh**, not a primitive Plane: `Art/Meshes/Water/RealisticWaterGrid.asset`
  (200x200 verts, generated by `RealisticWaterMeshGenerator`). A flat Plane (10x10 verts) has far
  too few vertices for the Gerstner vertex displacement to read.
- **Feature stack:**
  - 4-layer wind-coherent **Gerstner** vertex displacement with deep-water dispersion and exact
    derivative normals (GPU Gems 1, ch.1). Each wave = `(angle, wavelength, amplitude, speed
    multiplier)` + fold-safe steepness; `_WindDirection` and `_WindSpread` compress the authored
    angles around a shared wind direction without deleting crossing-wave energy.
    **One direction contract:** crests, micro ripples, and the temporal-foam advection all travel
    along `+_WindDirection`. That requires the Gerstner time term to be *subtracted* (GPU Gems
    prints `+ phi*t`, which sends the crest upwind). `EvaluateWave` in the shader and
    `AccumulateJacobian` in `RealisticWaterFoamUpdate.compute` must keep the same sign, or the
    injected whitecaps drift off the rendered crests.
  - Two deterministic seamless **micro-normal** maps sampled in mesh-anchored world space. Layer A
    follows `_WindDirection`; layer B uses a `32` degree wind offset, different tiling, and a
    different drift speed. Reoriented normal mapping combines both layers with the analytic
    Gerstner normal. Distance fade (`_DetailFadeStart/End`) and per-layer projected texel footprint
    fade remove detail before it becomes sub-pixel shimmer. Both 256x256 maps use mipmaps,
    trilinear filtering, repeat wrapping, and anisotropy 4; the shader performs two normal samples.
  - **Refraction:** distorted `_CameraOpaqueTexture` read with thickness scaling, an 8-percent
    screen-edge fade, clamped coordinates, and a reversed-Z-safe linear eye-depth test. Thin
    shoreline water approaches zero distortion, and foreground geometry is never pulled below the
    surface by steep displaced waves.
  - **Water optics:** `_CameraDepthTexture` reconstructs both the center and refracted opaque
    points. The unperturbed center ray and mean water level supply stable vertical and optical
    depth; the screen-space refraction offset affects only the sampled scene color and caustic
    location. This prevents neighboring terraces and wave troughs below shallow geometry from
    leaking the open-water fallback as moving green patches. Beer-Lambert transmittance uses
    `_AbsorptionCoefficients`; controlled in-scattering uses `_ScatteringColor` /
    `_ScatteringStrength`. Sky depth, invalid positions, geometry above mean water, foreground
    depth, and refraction outside the viewport use safe fallbacks.
  - **Reflection:** `RealisticWaterPlanarReflection` renders one mirrored, clipped camera for the
    lab water. Half resolution is the selected default; Full Resolution is diagnostic, and Sky
    Only disables the extra pass. The shader edge-fades local reflection and falls back to
    `GlossyEnvironmentReflection` (probe/skybox) whenever the component is unavailable. No global
    SSR renderer feature is installed. The reflect vector is clamped to the upper hemisphere so
    tilted facets never mirror the skybox's brown "ground" hemisphere. Reflection shares a
    Schlick energy split with transmission and is never multiplied by the main-light shadow.
  - **Lighting:** Cook-Torrance GGX direct sun reflection with Schlick Fresnel (`F0 = 0.0204`),
    correlated Smith visibility, a finite-sun roughness floor, and distance roughness increase.
    The retired `_SpecColor`, `_SpecPower`, and `_FresnelPower` material values are ignored.
  - **Foam:** `RealisticWaterTemporalFoam` updates a bounded world-space RGHalf history with a
    compute pass. R stores wind-advected, exponentially decaying Jacobian whitecaps; G stores a
    world-anchored shoreline band built from depth gradients, obstacle adjacency, and breakup
    noise. The shader samples both channels with independent crest/shore strengths. If compute
    history is disabled or unavailable, it falls back to the instantaneous Jacobian and
    water-column-depth masks.
  - **Caustics:** `RealisticWaterCausticProjection` enables nine bounded receiver overlays in the
    lab (five seabed terraces and four rocks). Their additive opaque-pass shader projects two
    animated world-space patterns along the main-light direction, then attenuates them by water
    depth, turbidity, sun angle, receiver normal, and main-light shadow. Pixels above the mean water
    plane or outside the water bounds are clipped. The water shader keeps its previous reconstructed
    seabed composite as the `SurfaceFallback` quality path.
  - **Underwater surface:** `RealisticWaterUnderwaterSurface` synchronizes the four Gerstner waves,
    normal layers, absorption, scattering, Fresnel, roughness, and reflection values to a second
    renderer that draws only back faces through `Cull Front`. The underside rebuilds and flips the
    displaced normal, blends transmission with strong total internal reflection near grazing
    angles, and shares its transition with `UnderwaterFogController`. A local CPU wave-height
    evaluation blends fog across a 0.4-unit crossing band instead of switching at the flat mean
    plane.
  - **Quality tiers:** `RealisticWaterQualityController` applies one coordinated feature budget.
    `High` selects half-resolution local reflection, 256x256 temporal foam, projected receiver
    caustics, and the underwater surface. `Low` selects sky/probe reflection, instantaneous foam,
    surface-composite caustics, and front-face-only rendering. The scene builder serializes `High`
    as the laboratory default.
  - **Shadows:** receives main-light shadows (rocks/terrain shadow the surface). Shadow affects the
    lit result and glint but **not** the body tint/transparency.
  - **Fog:** `multi_compile_fog` + `MixFog` on the final composite. The lab scene has fog off, but
    `Island.unity` uses linear fog from 250 m, and unfogged water reads as a hard seam against
    fogged terrain the moment this shader is promoted.
- **Transparent queue but effectively opaque output** (`ZWrite Off`, no `Blend` -> the shader does
  its own refraction and returns alpha=1). Renders after the opaque pass, which is why the opaque
  and depth textures contain the seabed.

#### Hard requirements / gotchas (realistic water)
- **PC URP asset must have the Camera Opaque Texture on.** `PC_RPAsset.asset` already had depth on;
  opaque was turned on by `Market/Debug/Water/Enable Opaque Texture (PC Pipeline)`
  (`RealisticWaterPipelineSetup`). Without it, refraction samples garbage. Mobile_RPAsset is
  intentionally left alone - this track is PC-only.
- **Planar reflection is local to WaterShaderLab.** The component disables water while rendering,
  and its camera requests no post processing, opaque color, depth texture, MSAA, or occlusion
  culling. Set its quality to Sky Only for the zero-extra-pass fallback.
  It renders for game cameras only; scene view, preview, and probe cameras clear
  `_PlanarReflectionAvailable` and fall back to the probe/sky reflection, so the surface never
  samples another camera's reflection. `_PlanarReflectionFlipY` is an `Auto/Never/Always` inspector
  toggle and **must stay `Never`** on this pipeline: URP 17 already normalises render-texture
  orientation, so `Auto` (flip when `SystemInfo.graphicsUVStartsAtTop`, i.e. always on D3D) mirrors
  the reflection and paints the seabed hemisphere over far water as a brown band. Verified by
  capture, not by assumption.
- **Temporal foam is local to WaterShaderLab.** The selected `History256` mode covers the water
  renderer's 100x100-unit world bounds with two 256x256 RGHalf history targets plus one R8
  shoreline mask (0.563 MiB total). Moving or resizing the water region by more than one history
  texel resets the buffers; moving the camera does not reproject or reset them. Set quality to
  `NoHistory` for the zero-buffer fallback.
- **Projected caustics are local to WaterShaderLab.** The receiver root redraws only the lab
  seabed/rock meshes and is bounded to the water renderer's XZ footprint. It adds no render target;
  the selected path costs nine simple additive receiver draws. Set
  `RealisticWaterCausticProjection` quality to `SurfaceFallback` to disable the root and restore the
  cheap water-surface composite.
- **The underside pass is optional.** Set `RealisticWaterUnderwaterSurface` quality to
  `FrontFaceOnly` to keep its renderer disabled while retaining the fog volume. The selected
  `UnderwaterSurface` path enables the child renderer only while the main camera is below the local
  displaced height, so it cannot leak dark back faces into above-water views.
- **Measure R8 separately.** `Market/Debug/Water/Capture R8 Underwater Performance` samples the
  front-face-only fallback and the enabled underside from one fixed underwater camera, then writes
  the explicit delta to `Artifacts/RealisticWater/R8/underwater_performance.md`.
- **Measure R9 by subsystem.** `Market/Debug/Water/Capture R9 Subsystem Profile` uses the R0
  1280x720 elevated camera to isolate the core fragment-dominated pass, opaque texture path, dense
  vertex grid, reflection, temporal foam, projected caustics, complete High tier, `MarketWater`
  reference, and a 360-degree High-tier camera turn.
- **Standalone gate:** `Market/Debug/Water/Build R9 Standalone Development` builds only
  `WaterShaderLab.unity`. Launch it with `-waterR9Capture -waterR9Output <folder>` to record the
  fixed High-tier screenshot and standalone timings before any production promotion.
- **Material serialization trap:** changing a shader's *default* property value does **not** change
  an existing `.mat` - the material keeps its own serialized copy. To change an existing material's
  values, use `modify_material` / rebuild it, not just a shader edit. (New properties added to the
  shader *do* fall back to shader defaults for materials that lack them.)
- The installer removes orphaned saved properties from all three realistic-water materials during
  migration. The R9 material audit reports zero undeclared texture, integer, float, or color
  entries.

#### Known limitation - not yet done
- **The candidate is intentionally not promoted.** R9 passed the technical laboratory gate, but
  `RealisticWater` is more physically detailed and more expensive to maintain than the stylized
  production options. Island already uses the independently packaged Bitgem water, and no explicit
  decision requested replacing it. `MarketWater.shader`, Bitgem water, and `RealisticWater.shader`
  remain independent, so rollback or a later promotion requires no shader reconstruction.

## Grass shader

### `Market/Nature/GrassWind`
- **Files:** `Assets/_Project/Art/Shaders/GrassWind.shader` + `GrassWindCommon.hlsl` (shared wind/
  wobble math).
- Alpha-cutout (`TransparentCutout`, `Cull Off`), toon-banded lighting with translucency/backlight
  and a rim glow. Vertex wind sway + "jelly" wobble/squash.
- **`_WindMaskFromUV` toggle** picks where the root-to-tip mask comes from: object-space Z for the
  geometry tufts (`Grass_1`/`Grass_2`), or `UV.y` for **flat cards** (whose object-space Z extent is
  0, so the Z path would collapse). `GrassCard.mat` needs this **on**.
- `_VertexColorTint` -> drop to 0 for meshes authored without a vertex-color set (they read black and
  would kill the tint); keep 1 where vertex color *is* the base tint.
- Built/scattered by `GrassCardBuilder` (menu `Market/Debug/Grass Card/1..4`).

## Iterating on water shaders

### R0 baseline

- Run `Market/Debug/Water/Capture R0 Baseline` before a realism pass. It captures three fixed
  1280x720 views under `Artifacts/RealisticWater/R0/`: elevated overview, shoreline detail, and
  near-horizon aliasing. Camera transforms are deterministic; animated wave phase remains live.
- Use `Market/Debug/Water/Capture R0 Views Only` for fast term isolation while tuning. It writes
  the same three views without entering Play Mode or replacing the existing performance report.
- The same command enters Play Mode, renders the elevated view to a fixed 1280x720 target, warms up
  for 2 seconds, samples for 8 seconds, then writes observed, CPU, and GPU frame-time statistics to
  `baseline.md`. Editor Play Mode numbers are comparative diagnostics, not a standalone-build
  performance guarantee; unavailable GPU timing is reported explicitly rather than inferred.
- `baseline.md` also records Unity/GPU/OS data, shader/material dependency hashes, exact camera
  transforms, and the material values that most strongly affect the current look.

1. Open `WaterShaderLab.unity` (rebuild it fresh with `Market/Debug/Build Water Shader Lab` -
   `WaterShaderLabSceneBuilder`). It has a terraced seabed (beach -> deep trench), foam-test rocks,
   the realistic water plane, sun + ambient, an instructions label, and the Player prefab.
   Fly with **F4** (Space / Left Ctrl = up/down) - see `DebugFlyMode`.
2. Edit the `.shader`, then `Assets/Refresh` **before** `recompile_scripts` when the file is new
   (a brand-new asset isn't imported yet, so the first compile can't see it).
3. Verify: `get_health_report` must be `ok` (shader errors show up as console errors, not compile
   failures). Then capture - either `Market/Debug/Capture Active Scene Camera` (edit mode) or
   `player_agent` (real Game View).
4. **Judge from a good angle.** A flat grazing eye-level view reads mostly as sky reflection
   regardless of foam/caustic/absorption settings (correct Fresnel, bad diagnostic angle). Use an
   elevated, downward-tilted view to judge foam and caustics, and a near-horizon view to check
   far-water aliasing.
5. **Isolate before concluding.** When tuning a term (foam, reflection, spec), zero the *other*
   terms via `modify_material` first - otherwise one effect's pixels get misread as another's. (This
   is exactly how a "verified" whitecap-foam feature once shipped completely dead: the visible white
   was shoreline foam + fresnel, and the Jacobian term was contributing zero.)

### Editor gotchas that bite during shader iteration
- Moving the Player via `set_transform` for a screenshot leaves the scene **dirty**; the next
  `Build Water Shader Lab` calls `SaveCurrentModifiedScenesIfUserWantsTo()` and pops a native
  "Save?" modal that blocks the Editor's main thread - every MCP call then times out until a human
  clicks it. To reset the player without that: `set_transform` back to spawn `(0, 1, -42)` identity
  and `save_scene`, or accept the dialog. `load_scene` on the already-active scene does **not**
  reliably discard in-memory transform edits.
